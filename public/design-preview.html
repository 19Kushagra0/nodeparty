🎬 NodeParty YouTube Watch Together Spec

Visual UI Design & Section Breakdown

Interactive design guide for the new Discord-style YouTube Watch Together workspace within NodeParty.

1

Full Workspace Layout Overview

This is how all elements come together inside the room workspace: Top search bar, active synchronized 16:9 cinema player, discovery video grid below it, and the shared Up-Next queue on the right.

🔍 1. Top Universal Search Bar

Instant query suggestions, direct YouTube link parsing, and one-click category filter pills (Trending, Lo-Fi Music, Gaming, Podcasts).

🍿 2. Synchronized Cinema Stage

16:9 4K player with ambient color backlight glow matching video scenes, synced scrubber, and Host-Authoritative playback sync.

⚡ 3. Concurrent Discovery Grid

Users can browse and explore videos without pausing or closing the currently playing stream.

📋 4. Shared Up-Next Queue

Democratic voting system where room members upvote videos to dynamically reorder what plays next.

2

Video Cards: "Watch Now" vs "Add to Queue"

Every video card in the discovery grid gives participants two clear, instant actions:

▶ Watch Now

Immediate Room Switch: Instantly replaces the active video and broadcasts the new stream to everyone in the room in real-time.

+ Add to Queue

Uninterrupted Queuing: Adds the video to the Up-Next queue quietly. Current playback continues without pause.

3

Interaction Flow Rules

Action Trigger What Happens
Search Type in search bar or click filter chip Grid updates immediately with results. Active video keeps playing.
Watch Now Click red "Watch Now" button Current video ends; new video starts for all room members immediately.
Queue Video Click "+ Add to Queue" button Pushed to Up-Next queue with 1 vote from sender. Playback does NOT stop.
Upvote Click ▲ on queued item Vote increments; list auto-sorts so most-popular videos play next.
Switch to Old UI Click Screen Share icon on left rail Switches back to standard screen-share / collaborative browser workspace.
